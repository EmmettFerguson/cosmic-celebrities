Saturn is the planet people picture when they picture a planet. Its rings, made mostly of water ice, reach nearly three-quarters of the way from Earth to the Moon.

It is also a lightweight. Saturn is the only planet [[less dense than water|Saturn's mean density is about 0.69 g/cm³, against 1.00 for water. Given a big enough bathtub, it would float. Source: [NASA Saturn facts](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/facts/)]], which makes its starring role all the stranger.
