# Credits

## Planet textures
Planet, Sun, Moon, Saturn-ring and Milky Way textures in `textures/` are from
**Solar System Scope** (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), released under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0).
They are based on NASA imagery and elevation data.

Ganymede, Ceres and Vesta reuse the Moon texture with a tint. Titan's hazy surface is
generated in code (`js/scene.js`, `titanTexture`).
